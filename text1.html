TVK

Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) is an Indian regional political party in Tamil Nadu and Puducherry, founded by actor Vijay on February 2, 2024. Headquartered in Panaiyur, Chennai, the party aims to contest the 2026 Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly elections, focusing on principles like social justice and secularism.
